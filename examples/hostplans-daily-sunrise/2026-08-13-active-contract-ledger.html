Use an active-contract ledger to resume agent work safely

A resumed session restores conversation. A five-field ledger restores the current outcome, authority, checkpoint, evidence, and stop rule.

Answer-first field guide Updated 2026-08-13

The operating rule

Resume for continuity, then re-read and revalidate the active contract before acting. A session can remember an old permission or checkpoint; the ledger makes both visible and reviewable.

The contract travels as one unit

Original Hostplan diagram. Explicit dimensions reserve the final layout.

The five-field ledger

Outcome

One observable result and explicit exclusions.

Authority

Allowed systems, data, tools, and actions.

Checkpoint

Last verified state and next safe action.

Evidence

Sources, commands, receipts, and failures.

Stop rule

When another retry becomes a human decision.

Resume workflow

  1. Restore context. Resume the intended session or open the canonical Hostplan URL.
  2. Re-observe. Check branch head, workspace state, external systems, and reviews.
  3. Revalidate authority. Confirm that the ledger still describes the permitted work.
  4. Continue one step. Take the next safe action, attach evidence, and update the checkpoint.

## Active contract

- Outcome: Patch the authorized parser and prove the regression is closed.
- Authority: repo A only; workspace write; no production access.
- Checkpoint: failing fixture reproduced at commit abc123.
- Evidence: issue link, failing command, verification receipt.
- Stop rule: ask before schema, dependency, network, or destructive changes.
